ADC Notebook Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026. This policy covers ADC Notebook for the Mac App Store.

Your notebooks stay under your control

Notebook text, drawings, attachments, recognition results, local versions and recovery copies are saved on your Mac. American Dev Corp does not collect this content through the app. The app has no advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs and requires no ADC account or paid subscription.

Optional file, web and nearby tools

You choose the files to import, export or share. Web clipping contacts the website you enter and may download images from that website or its providers; those services receive normal network information such as your IP address and follow their own policies. Nearby transfer is started by you and exchanges a display name and selected notebook files with the peer you approve. An optional self-hosted notebook connection communicates with the server you configure; American Dev Corp does not operate that server. No nearby discovery or hosted sync is required for local editing.

Device permissions

Microphone access is used only when you choose to record an audio attachment. Image and handwriting recognition uses system frameworks on your device. File access, native sharing, printing and Apple diagnostics follow your macOS permissions and Apple settings. The app does not automatically open other ADC apps or devices.

Retention and security

Notes, deleted items and saved page versions remain locally until you remove them. Password-protected section contents are encrypted locally; forgotten section passwords cannot be recovered. Exported copies and outside backups remain until you remove them separately. Back up important notebooks before removing app data.

Support

If you contact support, we receive the message and attachments you choose to send. Do not send private notebooks or passwords. Contact American Dev Corp using the contact information on our website. Policy changes will be reflected here.
