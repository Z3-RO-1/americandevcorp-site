ADC Canvas Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026. This policy covers the Mac App Store edition of ADC Canvas.

Your canvases stay on your Mac

ADC Canvas is a local canvas editor. Your canvases, notes, relationships, comments, attachments, workspace sessions and local recovery copies are stored on your Mac. American Dev Corp does not receive this content through the app. The Store edition has no account requirement, Codex connection, agent backend, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Files and sharing

The app requests access to files you choose to open or import. Imported attachments may be copied into the app’s local library. Exporting a canvas or review package creates files you can share yourself. A sharing service, printer, file provider or website you choose follows its own privacy policy. The app does not automatically access another ADC app or your other devices.

Retention and removal

Project saves, local recovery copies and attachments may remain until you remove them. Exported files and backups outside the app are not removed by uninstalling it. Keep independent backups before removing app data.

Support

If you contact American Dev Corp, we receive only the message and attachments you choose to send. Avoid sending private canvases, passwords or recovery keys. Apple diagnostics follow your Apple privacy settings.

For questions, use the support contact on AmericanDevCorp.com. Policy changes will be reflected here.
