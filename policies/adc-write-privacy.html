ADC Write Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026. This policy covers ADC Write for macOS.

Your documents stay under your control

ADC Write is a local document editor. Your documents, comments, tracked text edits, recovery drafts and saved versions are stored on your Mac and in locations you choose. American Dev Corp does not receive this content through the app. ADC Write does not require an ADC account, use an ADC sync server, or contain advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

File permissions and preferences

The Mac App Store edition requests access to files or folders you select. It stores security-scoped bookmarks locally so it can reopen authorized locations after you restart the app. Editing preferences, recent-file references, document metadata, file permissions and local versions remain on your Mac. Granting a folder permits document management inside that folder; it does not upload its contents.

Sharing and other services

Export creates a file copy. When you choose a sharing service, storage provider, printer, or external link, that service may receive the content you select under its own privacy terms. ADC Write has no automatic connection to ADC Files; you choose when to export and import independent files.

Recovery, retention and removal

Native documents use .fwrite. Recovery drafts, immutable saved versions and ADC Write Trash can retain previous contents after you edit or move a document. Trash does not automatically purge. Files in other folders, backups or storage services are not removed simply by uninstalling ADC Write. You control retention and any additional backups.

Support and Apple diagnostics

If you contact support, we receive the email, message and attachments you choose to send and use them to respond. Do not include private documents, passwords or recovery keys unless you intentionally choose to share them. macOS diagnostics and any diagnostic information you share with developers follow your Apple privacy settings.
