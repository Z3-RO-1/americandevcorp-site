Family Passport Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 30, 2026

Family Passport is designed to help users organize family records such as medical, dental, vision, insurance, facility, identification, and contact information in one place.

Information handled by the app

Family Passport may store information that you enter about yourself or family members, including names, addresses, phone numbers, policy numbers, identification details, and related family record information.

How information is stored

Based on the current app design, data is intended to remain on your device so you can access your family records from your handheld device. This policy should be updated if cloud sync, account systems, analytics, or third-party services are added later.

Data sharing

Family Passport does not sell personal information. Data entered into the app is not shared with outside parties except as required to provide app functionality or comply with legal obligations.

Security

Family Passport is intended to keep important family records more accessible and organized on your personal device. Users are responsible for securing access to their device with passcodes, biometrics, or other device protections.

Children and family data

Because the app may contain information about family members, including children, users should only enter and manage information they are authorized to maintain.

Policy updates

This privacy policy may be updated as the app changes. If data practices change materially, this page should be revised before new versions are submitted for review.
