ADC Files Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026. This policy covers ADC Files for Mac and iPhone.

Your files stay under your control

ADC Files stores your library on your device and in recovery locations you select. American Dev Corp does not receive your file contents through the app. The app has no advertising or tracking SDK and does not require an ADC cloud account.

Nearby Sync

When you enable nearby discovery, the app advertises a device label on the local network. Selected files, saved versions and necessary metadata, including filenames, folder relationships and tags, are exchanged with devices you pair and approve. Pairing uses code confirmation and encrypted transfers. Device identity keys are stored in the device Keychain; saved pairing information stays locally. No American Dev Corp sync server handles these transfers.

Permissions and controls

The app requests local-network access for nearby sync and access to files or folders you select. It does not automatically read your system Photos library or Contacts. Stop nearby discovery, change selections, or remove saved pairings from the app. Unpairing does not erase received files. Deletions remain local, and saved versions or recovery copies can retain earlier contents until you remove them.

Support, sharing and diagnostics

If you contact support, we receive the email, message and attachments you choose to send and use them to respond. Sharing files, opening websites, or using an external storage provider is governed by the service you choose. Apple diagnostics and any diagnostic information shared with developers follow your Apple privacy settings.

Retention and security

You manage your local library and recovery copies. Removing the app does not necessarily remove backups on other drives. Transfers are encrypted; storage protection also depends on your device and drive settings. No system can guarantee against every loss or unauthorized access.
