The Market Directory Privacy Policy

The Market Directory is designed to help consumers discover storefronts and contact sellers directly. Information provided in the app may be used to support account access, storefront requests, support requests, messaging, directory listings, reviews, upvotes, and administrative review.

Information users may provide

  • Consumer account details such as name, email address, date of birth, and sign-in credentials.
  • Store Host request details such as business name, owner or contact name, email address, category, city, state, and business description.
  • Support request details submitted through app support forms.
  • Messages, reviews, upvotes, storefront details, product details, and other content submitted through the app.

How information is used

  • To operate app accounts, storefront discovery, directory filtering, messaging, reviews, and support workflows.
  • To review Store Host access requests and issue one-time access codes when approved.
  • To help administrators respond to support requests, enforce community guidelines, and maintain marketplace quality.
  • To improve app reliability, beta testing, and marketplace operations.

Data sharing

American Dev Corp does not sell user personal information. Information may be reviewed by the developer or authorized administrators when needed for support, safety, account access, storefront review, or policy enforcement.

Direct seller communication

The Market Directory is a message-first marketplace directory. Buyers and sellers may share information with each other through app messaging when they choose to ask questions, request details, or arrange a purchase.
