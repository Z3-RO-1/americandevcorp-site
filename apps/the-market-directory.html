App

The Market Directory

The Market Directory is a live iOS app for discovering local and independent storefronts through a structured marketplace directory. The app is designed around direct seller communication rather than anonymous checkout: consumers browse products and storefronts, then contact the business to ask questions, request details, or arrange a purchase directly.

Current build context

  • Status: Live on the App Store.
  • Developer: Gilbert Aguirre / American Dev Corp.
  • Purpose: Provide a professional vendor directory with storefront discovery, product browsing, and direct seller messaging.
  • Audience: Consumers looking for vendors, services, and small businesses; store hosts managing storefront listings and product requests; administrators reviewing access and support activity.
  • Commerce model: Purchases are not completed through in-app checkout. Buyers and sellers coordinate through individual messages.
  • App Store listing: The Market Directory on the App Store.

Core features

  • Featured storefront and product discovery.
  • Directory browsing by category with state and city filters.
  • Storefront pages with product listings, reviews, and consumer upvotes.
  • Consumer account creation with agreement and password validation.
  • Store Host account flow gated by admin-issued email and one-time access code.
  • Admin tools for host access requests, support requests, contacts, analytics, and promotional banners.
  • Message-first inquiry flow for contacting storefronts about products or services.
