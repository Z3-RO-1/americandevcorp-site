App

Family Passport

Family Passport is a family records command center for the household member who manages everyone’s important information in one place.

What it does

The app helps organize medical, dental, vision, insurance, facility, and identification details for multiple family members so the right addresses, phone numbers, policy numbers, and card details are available when they are needed.

It also presents family relationships visually in a tree structure, including immediate family and extended family branches connected through maternal and paternal sides.
