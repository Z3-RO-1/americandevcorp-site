App

On-site

On-site is a parking operations tool for apartment complexes and property managers to verify whether vehicles belong on the property, which spaces they are assigned to, and when alerts should be sent before towing or enforcement action.

What it does

The app is designed to help property owners and authorized subcontractors identify vehicles by resident, assigned parking space, vehicle description, license plate, and owner contact details.

It is intended to support parking enforcement decisions, including whether a vehicle should be left alone, flagged, contacted, or towed, while also alerting the property owner about actions taken on site.
